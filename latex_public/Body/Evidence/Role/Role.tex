\subsubsection{Municipal State Enterprise “iKOMEK109” City Monitoring and Rapid Response Center}
\label{subsubsec:RoleIKomek}

iKOMEK109 serves as the capital’s single monitoring and rapid-response hub. The Deputy Mayor explains that it functions as the city’s unified Situation Center, stating that it is \emph{"a single contact center for vital city services 'iKOMEK109' (handling more than 2.2 million requests per year, an analogue of 3-1-1 in the United States), a Situation Center where city cameras, fire-extinguishing systems and Internet-of-Things solutions are integrated for round-the-clock monitoring of safety and life-support, and an Analytics Center that works with Big Data to identify systemic problems and support decision-making."} \ExhibitRef{IKomekDeputyAkimLetter}

Independent public records corroborate iKOMEK109’s citywide mandate and scale, including its status as a unified 24/7 intake point, service catalog and official channels. \ExhibitRef{IKomekGov,IKomekSiteShot,IKomekPress}

The Deputy Mayor confirms that \emph{"Askar Taximov has been the Head of the City Monitoring and Rapid Response Center 'iKOMEK109' since 27 May 2022"} and notes that \emph{"during his work he has proven himself as a highly professional and effective leader who makes significant efforts in digitalization, technological transformation and innovative development of the company."} \ExhibitRef{IKomekDeputyAkimLetter}

The Deputy Mayor attributes multiple quantified operational improvements to methods and governance practices implemented under Mr.~Taximov’s leadership. The letter notes that his prioritization and decision-matrix approach \emph{"allowed us to reduce the number of current problems classified as 'especially important' and 'very urgent' from 112 to 27, that is by 76\%."} It records that in 2023 \emph{"426,542 requests were closed, which is 28.4\% more than in 2022"} and that \emph{"from 1 July 2022, 31 systemic problems were closed, more than 80 children's playgrounds were modernized and 43 sports grounds were reconstructed."} \ExhibitRef{IKomekDeputyAkimLetter}

The letter also describes public-safety monitoring outcomes tied to Situation Center operations, including that \emph{"the number of surveillance cameras was increased from 9,752 to 10,581 (by 8.5\%)"} and that process changes in monitoring and enforcement coordination led to \emph{"an increase of 17\% in registered cases of detaining distributors of narcotic and synthetic substances using video surveillance compared to the previous year."} It reports that iKOMEK109 \emph{"assisted in solving 23 criminal offenses and 19,384 administrative offenses, of which 13,015 were formalized by protocol,"} and that \emph{"52,008,750 tenge of fines were paid into the budget."} \ExhibitRef{IKomekDeputyAkimLetter}

On internal performance and social policy, the Deputy Mayor states that Mr.~Taximov \emph{"from 1 June 2022 to the present increased wages for production staff by 51\% and for administrative and managerial staff by 31\%, and developed a system of material incentives and compensation under which each rank-and-file employee from 1 June 2022 receives a bonus of up to four salaries based on annual KPI results."} His youth-practice and inclusion programs \emph{"involved more than 60 people"} and \emph{"increased the number of employees with disabilities from 6 to 10,"} and the letter notes that \emph{"thanks to the developed program he was able to bring iKOMEK109 into the category of 'the best employers of the capital' in 2023 for hiring young specialists and workers from socially vulnerable groups of the population."} \ExhibitRef{IKomekDeputyAkimLetter}

His creation of an IT and research-and-development department and data-science workflows is credited with improving electoral data quality. The letter explains that the department \emph{"on an ongoing basis carries out actualization of the voter list in Astana by identifying incorrect or outdated registration addresses and assigning them an address registration code"} and that this approach \emph{"ensured an increase in the actuality of the voter register from 84\% to 100\%."} It notes that in 2023 \emph{"geolocations were added for 513 building addresses with a total of 25,036 voters"} and that \emph{"more than 1,000 addresses with abolished or incorrect address codes were corrected."} \ExhibitRef{IKomekDeputyAkimLetter}

The Deputy Mayor summarizes that \emph{"his competent leadership, strategic vision and creative thinking played a key and essential role in the development and digital transformation of the iKOMEK109 center, significantly improving the quality of services and interaction with residents of the city."} \ExhibitRef{IKomekDeputyAkimLetter}

\subsubsection{Joint Stock Company “State Technical Service” National Cybersecurity Operator}
\label{subsubsec:RoleSTS}

The State Technical Service (STS) is the national cybersecurity operator charged with protecting the electronic government systems and critical information and communication infrastructure of Kazakhstan. Its functions include security testing of government information systems, monitoring of national networks and support for KZ-CERT’s incident-prevention work. \ExhibitRef{STSRoleLetter,STSSiteShot1,STSSiteShot2}

The STS letter confirms that \emph{"Askar Boranbayevich Taximov has been working as an Independent Director and member of the Board of Directors of the Joint Stock Company 'State Technical Service' since 18 January 2022 on the basis of an order of the Chairman of the National Security Committee of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 14 January 2022."} It specifies that \emph{"in his work Askar Taximov implements innovative projects and coordinates the company’s areas in information and cybersecurity, and also heads the Strategic Development Committee of the Board of Directors."} \ExhibitRef{STSRoleLetter}

First, the letter states that \emph{"Taximov A.B. was engaged in the implementation of a Telegram bot for monitoring mass media, intended to automate the processes of searching for news concerning information security by certain tags from official internet resources and Telegram channels of the media,"} and confirms that \emph{"this Telegram bot was implemented for STS subdivisions and is used in everyday activities."} \ExhibitRef{STSRoleLetter}

Second, it records that \emph{"Taximov A.B. participated in the development of the Synaq internet portal, implemented to automate the process of providing testing services for 'electronic government' informatization objects and information systems classified as critical information and communication infrastructure for compliance with information security requirements,"} emphasizing that \emph{"development of Synaq made it possible to automate the testing process, significantly reduce paperwork and it is used in the activities of STS."} \ExhibitRef{STSRoleLetter}

Third, in 2023 STS carried out work on the development of \emph{"the ScanKZ search system for collecting information on the security level of devices connected to the internet in the Kazakhstan segment of the internet,"} where the letter specifies that \emph{"Taximov A.B. played a key role in the development of the system’s components."} ScanKZ is described as software \emph{"intended for automatic searching for information on the security level of devices (open ports, device versions, services and the presence of vulnerabilities) by IP addresses and domain names."} As a result, \emph{"the current activities of KZ-CERT in preventing computer security threats were improved through automatic identification of vulnerable objects in the network and the ability to assess the security status of devices in Kazakhstan and worldwide connected to the internet."} \ExhibitRef{STSRoleLetter}

Summarizing his contribution, the letter concludes that \emph{"in performing the above tasks, Taximov A.B. demonstrated a high level of analytical thinking and professionalism; his technical knowledge and innovative approach made it possible to achieve the set goals and, in general, increase the efficiency of STS activities in these areas."} \ExhibitRef{STSRoleLetter}

\subsubsection{Limited Liability Partnership “Elorda Qurylys Kompaniyasy” Municipal Developer}
\label{subsubsec:RoleEQK}

Elorda Qurylys Kompaniyasy (EQK) is the municipal developer created to resolve problem shared-equity construction projects and complete stalled residential complexes so that equity holders receive their apartments. Public materials document EQK’s mandate and delivered complexes, corroborating its prominent role in Astana’s housing policy and urban development. \ExhibitRef{EQKRoleLetter,EQKSiteShot1,EQKInstagramShot}

The CEO’s letter states that \emph{"during his period of activity, from March to June 2022, Askar held the position of First Deputy General Director for financial and IT matters"} and that in this short period \emph{"he proved himself as a highly qualified manager, possessing the ability to find non-standard, innovative solutions and to introduce modern approaches into the company’s work."} \ExhibitRef{EQKRoleLetter}

On financing, the CEO records that \emph{"Askar succeeded in obtaining funding totaling 51 billion tenge from the National Fund of the Republic of Kazakhstan and the local budget"} and that \emph{"thanks to the attracted funds it was possible to finance the completion of problem sites in the city of Astana."} These funds \emph{"made it possible to successfully complete work on 10 problem sites, including 27 residential buildings, and as a result more than 3,000 families received the keys to their long-awaited apartments."} \ExhibitRef{EQKRoleLetter}

On controls and analytics, the CEO writes that \emph{"he developed and implemented in the company a digital financial model, which ensured transparency and targeted allocation of funds from the National Fund of the Republic of Kazakhstan, the republican and local budgets"} and that he \emph{"used the 1C:ERP information platform"} with multiple planning levels and a \emph{"'Target Indicators Monitor' with key financial metrics for each level."} Before this model, \emph{"weak coordination between departments and inefficient cost management led to annual losses of 10\% of the total budget for residential construction, which amounted to 523 million tenge."} After implementation, \emph{"the company reduced these costs by 30\%, which led to savings of 156.9 million tenge per year,"} while the approach \emph{"made it possible to reduce the average time for commissioning sites by 11\% – from 24 months to 21 months,"} and \emph{"accelerated workflows, which led to a 7–9\% increase in profit."} \ExhibitRef{EQKRoleLetter}

The CEO further describes operational modernization and transparency measures, stating that a key task was \emph{"the introduction of automation at shared-equity construction sites"} through remote video monitoring and Computer Vision-enabled transparency for equity holders. The letter states that \emph{"construction rates increased significantly, which made it possible to reduce the commissioning time of sites by 3 months,"} that \emph{"the introduction of online viewing reduced the number of complaints from equity holders by 35\%,"} and that \emph{"the number of thefts at construction sites decreased by 46\%,"} with occupational safety and discipline indicators improving \emph{"by an average factor of 2.3."} \ExhibitRef{EQKRoleLetter}

Finally, the letter credits him with introducing Building Information Modeling, noting that \emph{"for adapting this technology to the needs of our company Askar developed a clear algorithm for implementing BIM, which included ten key stages"} and that this \emph{"made it possible to improve the quality of design, reducing errors and risks during project review by a factor of 3.5 in the authorized bodies, as well as to save time at the implementation stage."} \ExhibitRef{EQKRoleLetter}

The CEO concludes that \emph{"his contribution to the development of the company was invaluable"} and that he \emph{"played a key role in the technological transformation of the company."} \ExhibitRef{EQKRoleLetter}

\subsubsection{Joint Stock Company “Astana Innovations” Smart City Operator}
\label{subsubsec:RoleAIN}

Astana Innovations is the city’s designated Smart City operator, responsible for the Big Data platform, the Smart Astana portal and mobile application, the transition from open data to open APIs, and the operation of the underground cable duct network that underpins high-speed internet and telecom services. Public materials corroborate its role as a core urban digital-services operator. \ExhibitRef{AINRoleLetter,AINSiteShot,AINInstagramShot}

The Astana Innovations letter explains that \emph{"Askar, having studied the current state of the product as well as the infrastructure of local executive bodies, immediately identified the potential that lies in deep analysis and use of these data to improve urban infrastructure and ensure a comfortable life for residents."} It notes that \emph{"he personally initiated further improvement of the platform capable of processing and analyzing large volumes collected from various city sources, about 76 databases"} and that \emph{"with the help of Askar’s unique knowledge and efforts, 12 analytical data models were implemented and an additional text-and-graphics visualization block was created."} \ExhibitRef{AINRoleLetter}

Among these, the letter highlights that \emph{"thanks to Askar’s innovative ideas, a model was developed and implemented to identify residential properties in which the number of officially registered residents exceeds 10 people ('rubber apartments')"} and that \emph{"a special achievement under Askar’s leadership was the construction of a 'Profile of large families'."} In this work \emph{"he determined, from government data sources, a list of large families in the city totaling 14,601 families"} and \emph{"the number of children amounted to 92,957."} \ExhibitRef{AINRoleLetter}

The Big Data platform is described as \emph{"capable of processing and analyzing large volumes collected from various city sources, about 76 databases."} The letter states that \emph{"the developed system made it possible to combine 104 data layers (from 76 databases), integrated and displayed on the city’s digital map"} and that \emph{"the web portal provides 20 datasets in 9 categories (education, healthcare, transport, housing and utilities, city services, internet, ecology, news and events)." } \ExhibitRef{AINRoleLetter}

On telecom infrastructure, the letter records that \emph{"Astana Innovations is designated as the managing company of the city for operation of the cable duct system"} and notes that \emph{"according to the results of 2019, the company concluded 20 contracts for leasing the cable duct system, which brought income of 38 million tenge."} After Mr.~Taximov’s reforms, \emph{"the number of contracts increased from 20 to 73 and total income rose from 38 million tenge to 152 million tenge in 2021, thereby in a short period turning the city’s cable duct operations from 'loss-making' to 'profitable'"} and \emph{"this achievement more than triples the indicators of 2019."} \ExhibitRef{AINRoleLetter}

The letter also credits Mr.~Taximov with improvements in electoral and population data, stating that \emph{"the indicator of the actuality of the voter register in Astana increased by 15.6 percentage points: if as of 1 January 2020 it was 84.4\%, then as of 1 December 2020 it was 100\%."} It further notes that he \emph{"updated address information for more than 50,000 citizens"} and \emph{"assigned geolocations for more than 8,000 building addresses on the city map."} \ExhibitRef{AINRoleLetter}

Summarizing his contribution, the CEO writes that \emph{"the above results and achieved successes were obtained thanks to the professional efforts, experience and high expertise of Askar Taximov, as well as his persistence and innovative solutions"} and expresses \emph{"deep gratitude"} for his \emph{"significant contribution"} to the company and the Smart Astana project. \ExhibitRef{AINRoleLetter}
