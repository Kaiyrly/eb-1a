\subsection{Comparable evidence of public display of work}

\label{subsec:Display}

Under 8 C.F.R. \S~204.5(h)(4), when a criterion in \S~204.5(h)(3), such as artistic exhibitions or showcases, does not directly apply to the occupation, USCIS may consider comparable evidence. IT-related work and applied research are not typically presented in artistic exhibitions. Instead, professionals in this field publicly display their work through technical conference presentations, scholarly papers, and industry forums, where project architectures, methodologies, and implementation case studies are presented and archived in citable proceedings or repositories. Therefore, the materials below demonstrate evidence of comparable public display.

\bigskip

\noindent\underline{Participation in the conference ``Digitalization and technological revolutions: current challenges} \\
\noindent\underline{and opportunities'', 12 July 2025.} \\
The work includes the paper ``How to detect multi-level cyber threats in real time with AI and behavioral pattern analysis''. The paper presents an author-developed artificial intelligence–based detection methodology for real-time identification of complex, multi-level cyber threats using behavioral pattern analysis instead of static signature matching. It proposes a system architecture and detection logic that analyze behavioral deviations across multiple layers to identify coordinated and previously unknown attack patterns. The paper also reports an experimental evaluation comparing the proposed AI-based approach with traditional detection methods, demonstrating its effectiveness in identifying sophisticated threats in dynamic environments. The conference was organized by the International Centre for Innovative Research ``Omega Science,'' and the proceedings were published as a citable volume available through academic libraries.

\bigskip

\noindent\underline{Participation in the International Scientific practical conference ``World science: Problems and} \\
\noindent\underline{Innovations'', 30 May 2025.} \\
The work includes the paper ``Algorithm for Developing post-quantum cryptography to protect critical infrastructure against quantum attacks''. The paper proposes a lattice-based cryptographic approach designed to improve resilience against future quantum-computing threats. It outlines the mathematical model, algorithmic structure, and performance characteristics in comparison with existing cryptographic schemes. The conference was organized by the International Centre for Scientific Cooperation ``Science and Education'' and the proceedings were publicly released with bibliographic records.

\bigskip

\noindent\underline{Participation in the conference ``Modern technologies in technical and natural sciences'',} \\
\noindent\underline{5 June 2025.} \\
At the conference, A. Taximov presented the work ``Optimization of cloud system performance using adaptive machine learning algorithms.''. The paper addresses the practical problem of maintaining stable cloud performance under rapidly changing workloads and proposes applying adaptive ML methods to automate key optimization decisions, including workload forecasting, resource auto scaling, database query optimization, and anomaly detection, thereby reducing manual tuning and performance degradation. The conference was organized and proceedings were published as a citable volume by the International Institute for Advanced Studies named after Lomonosov.

\bigskip

\noindent\underline{Participation in the National Scientific practical conference ``Modern methods and innovations} \\
\noindent\underline{in science,'', 12 May 2025.} \\
The work includes the paper ``Mechanism of accelerated transfer of technologies from scientific laboratories to industry of Kazakhstan''. The conference proceedings identify the included materials as selected scientific works recommended for publication by the editorial and publishing council of the Humanitarian National Research Institute ``Natsrazvitie.'' The paper presents an author-developed mechanism for accelerating technology transfer in Kazakhstan and proposes a model approach built around the creation and integration of the Technology Transfer Ecosystem Platform. This proposed platform model is designed to reduce barriers to commercialization by structurally linking science, industry, and the state within a single ecosystem. The paper outlines the stages of technology transfer, defines the functions of key participants, and specifies the infrastructure and regulatory conditions required to implement the model, with the expected result of faster and more coordinated commercialization of scientific developments in Kazakhstan. The conference was organized by the Humanitarian National Research Institute ``Natsrazvitie''.

\bigskip

\noindent\underline{Participation in the International scientific practical conference ``Scientific Forum: Innovative} \\
\noindent\underline{Science'', No. 6\_(85), 28 June 2025.} \\
The work includes the paper ``Methodology of data transfer protocol standardization for cross-platform integration of IoT devices''. The paper proposes a standardized protocol framework for interoperable communication among heterogeneous IoT devices. It describes protocol architecture, integration mechanisms, and system-level efficiency improvements. The conference proceedings list the paper in the official table of contents, and the electronic version is archived in national scientific repositories and academic databases, confirming its formal publication and public availability. The conference was organized by the International Centre for Innovative Research and Publishing ``MCNO'' and published as part of a citable international scientific proceedings volume (ISSN 2542-1255).

\bigskip

\noindent\underline{Participation in the Forum ``Profit Contact Day 2025: New technologies in contact centers'',} \\
\noindent\underline{13 June 2025.} \\
The work includes a report and presentation titled ``Digital transformation of a city platform: experience of creating iKOMEK 2.0''. The official program and event materials document the public presentation of his work, including coverage of his on-stage presentation. The presentation publicly showcased the architecture and implementation approach for upgrading a municipal contact-center platform into iKOMEK 2.0, including digital workflow redesign, omnichannel service logic, and modernization of the city platform’s operational model for citizen requests. The forum was held in Almaty at the InterContinental hotel in offline and online format, with 200+ offline participants and 1000+ online viewers, and focused on innovative technologies for contact centers, including AI and automation.

\bigskip

\noindent\underline{Participation in the conference ``Profit Smart City Day 2024'', 11 October 2024.} \\
The work includes presentation with a title ``Smart City: The role of the iKOMEK109 situation center in ensuring city safety and identifying housing and utility problems''. The presentation materials, together with an official invitation letter and photographic evidence, document the public display of the work. The presentation demonstrated the architecture and operational model of the iKOMEK109 situation center, including AI-assisted monitoring, integration of large-scale video infrastructure, and automated coordination of municipal services. The event was organized by Profit Events and focused on urban digital transformation in Kazakhstan.

\bigskip

\noindent\underline{Participation in the conference ``Profit Education Day 2023'', 20 October 2023 dedicated to the} \\
\noindent\underline{digitalization of education in Kazakhstan.} \\
Mr. Taximov delivered a presentation titled ``iKOMEK109: education, IT, and urban development,'' in which he presented the applied use of urban digital platforms in the education sector. The presentation demonstrated how the iKOMEK109 situation center architecture is used to monitor, analyze, and respond to education-related incidents, including citizen requests concerning schools, kindergartens, and educational infrastructure. The report showcased real operational data, omni-channel citizen interaction, analytics dashboards, and interdepartmental coordination mechanisms, illustrating the practical integration of city IT systems into education governance. The public display of the work is documented through the official conference program, speaker listings, presentation materials, and photographic evidence of the presentation. The event was organized by Profit Events as part of the annual Profit Education Day forum and brought together representatives of government bodies, universities, IT companies, and education-sector decision-makers, focusing on digital transformation, EdTech solutions, cloud platforms, and information security in education.

\bigskip

\noindent\underline{Participation in the conference ``Profit Smart City Day 2023,'' 15 September 2023.} \\
The work includes a public presentation titled ``iKOMEK109 today''. The presentation materials, together with the official conference program, speaker listings, and video recordings, document the public display of Mr. Taximov’s work. The presentation demonstrated the architecture, operational model, and real-world deployment of the iKOMEK109 city situation center, including centralized intake of citizen requests, integration with municipal and emergency services, large-scale video surveillance infrastructure, and data-driven coordination of city services. The presentation highlighted practical results of digital city governance, including improved incident detection, service response coordination, and analytics-supported decision-making at the municipal level. The event was focused on digitalization of cities, smart-city infrastructure, video analytics, urban safety, and automated public-service management, with participation from government regulators, city administrations, and industry experts.

\bigskip

\noindent\underline{Participation in the Digital Festival ``Google DevFest Taraz 2023'', 16--17 November 2023.} \\
The work includes the presentation and technical case study ``City center for monitoring and operational response iKOMEK109 and project QalaQyzmet''. The case study describes the design and implementation of a unified digital platform integrating municipal utility services into a single window online ``One Stop Shop''. The presentation demonstrated how the iKOMEK109 platform integrates multiple municipal services such as housing and utilities, incident reporting, service requests, and notifications within one coordinated digital interface. The proposed ``one-stop shop'' model enables residents to submit requests, track resolution status, and receive updates across multiple city services through a single entry point, eliminating fragmentation and reducing response time. The case study covered system architecture, inter-service integration, secure data exchange, and automated user-notification mechanisms, illustrating how disparate municipal systems are orchestrated into a coherent operational workflow. The event was Organized by the Governance of Zhambyl Region in partnership with Google Developers Group and Zhambyl Hub. The goal was to foster regional digitalization and IT talent.

\bigskip

Across these events, Mr. Taximov’s own developed digital platforms, system architectures, and applied implementation methodologies were publicly presented at scientific and industry conferences and documented in official programs, proceedings, and event materials. Because traditional artistic exhibitions do not apply to IT project management, these public conference presentations constitute comparable evidence of public display under 8 C.F.R. \S~204.5(h)(4).
