\subsection{Comparable evidence of public display of work}

\label{subsec:Display}

Under 8~C.F.R.\ §\,204.5(h)(4), when a criterion in §\,204.5(h)(3), such as artistic exhibitions or showcases, does not directly apply to the occupation, USCIS may consider comparable evidence. IT project management and applied research are not typically presented in artistic exhibitions. Instead, professionals in this field publicly display their work through technical conference presentations, scholarly papers, and industry forums, where project architectures, methodologies, and implementation case studies are presented and archived in citable proceedings or repositories. The materials below therefore demonstrate evidence of comparable public display.

\medskip

\underline{Participation in the conference ``Digitalization and Technological Revolutions: Current Challenges and Opportunities'' (Omega Science), 12 July 2025.}  
The work includes the paper \emph{``How to Detect Multi-Level Cyber Threats in Real Time with AI and Behavioral Pattern Analysis''} (authored by A.~Taximov, 12 July 2025).  
The paper presents an artificial intelligence–based approach for real-time detection of complex cyber threats using behavioral analysis rather than static signature matching. It describes system architecture, detection logic, and experimental evaluation comparing AI-based detection with traditional methods. The conference was organized by the International Centre for Innovative Research ``Omega Science,'' and the proceedings were published as a citable volume available through academic libraries.

\medskip

\underline{Participation in the LXXXIII International Scientific Practical Conference ``World Science: Problems and Innovations,'' 30 May 2025.}  
The work includes the paper \emph{``Algorithm for Developing Post-Quantum Cryptography to Protect Critical Infrastructure against Quantum Attacks (LatticeCI).''}  
The paper proposes a lattice-based cryptographic approach designed to improve resilience against future quantum-computing threats. It outlines the mathematical model, algorithmic structure, and performance characteristics in comparison with existing cryptographic schemes. The conference was organized by the International Centre for Scientific Cooperation ``Science and Education,'' and the proceedings were publicly released with bibliographic records.

\medskip

\underline{Participation in the conference ``Modern Technologies in Technical and Natural Sciences,'' 5 June 2025.}  
At the conference, A.~Taximov presented the work \emph{``Optimization of Cloud System Performance Using Adaptive Machine Learning Algorithms.''}  
The paper examines the application of machine learning techniques to cloud-system load forecasting, automatic resource scaling, anomaly detection, and data optimization. The presentation focused on system stability, performance forecasting, and automation methods relevant to large-scale infrastructure environments.

\medskip

\underline{Participation in the XLIII All-Russian Scientific Practical Conference ``Modern Methods and Innovations in Science,'' May 2025.}  
The work includes the paper \emph{``Mechanism of Accelerated Technology Transfer from Scientific Laboratories to the Industry of Kazakhstan (TTEP).''}  
The conference proceedings identify the included materials as selected scientific works recommended by the Editorial and Publishing Council. The paper presents a framework for a national digital platform intended to facilitate structured technology transfer between research laboratories and private industry, including workflow design, intellectual-property handling, and commercialization pathways.

\medskip

\underline{Participation in the LXXXV International Scientific Practical Conference Series ``Scientific Forum: Innovative Science,'' Issue~6(85), 2025.}  
The work includes the paper \emph{``Methodology of Data Transfer Protocol Standardization for Cross-Platform Integration of IoT Devices''} (A.~Taximov, Issue~6(85), 2025).  
The paper proposes a standardized protocol framework for interoperable communication among heterogeneous IoT devices. It describes protocol architecture, integration mechanisms, and system-level efficiency improvements. The proceedings list the paper in the issue contents, and electronic versions are archived in national scientific repositories.

\medskip

\underline{Participation in the conference ``Profit Smart City Day 2024,'' 11 October 2024.}  
The work includes a report and presentation titled \emph{``Smart City: The Role of the iKOMEK109 Situation Center in Ensuring City Safety and Identifying Housing and Utility Problems''} (presented by A.~Taximov, 11 October 2024).  
The presentation materials, together with an official invitation letter and photographic evidence, document the public display of the work. The presentation demonstrated the architecture and operational model of the iKOMEK109 situation center, including AI-assisted monitoring, integration of large-scale video infrastructure, and automated coordination of municipal services. The event was organized by Profit Events and focused on urban digital transformation in Kazakhstan.

\medskip

\underline{Participation in the Digital Festival ``Google DevFest Taraz 2023,'' 16--17 November 2023.}  
The work includes the presentation and technical case study \emph{``City Center for Monitoring and Operational Response iKOMEK109 and Project QalaQyzmet (IQALA)''} (presented by A.~Taximov, 16 November 2023).  
The case study describes the design and implementation of a unified digital platform integrating municipal utility services into a single online interface. It addresses system architecture, service integration, secure data exchange, and user-notification mechanisms. The event was Organized by the Governance of Zhambyl Region in partnership with Google Developers Group and Zhambyl Hub. The goal was to foster regional digitalization and IT talent.

\medskip

Across these items, Mr.~Taximov’s own professional work products were publicly presented at scientific and industry conferences and archived in official proceedings, reports, and repositories. Because traditional artistic exhibitions do not apply to technical and programmatic leadership in information technology, these presentations constitute comparable evidence of public display under 8~C.F.R.\ §\,204.5(h)(4).
