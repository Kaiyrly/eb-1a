\subsection{Evidence of \mrls authorship of scholarly articles in the field, in professional or major trade publications or other major media}

\label{subsec:Articles}

\smallskip

Mr.~Taximov has authored 13 scholarly and professional articles in VAK-reviewed journals. The topics of these publications include smart-city analytics, municipal data engineering, open data and APIs, cybersecurity, and civic IT productization.

The Higher Attestation Commission (VAK) under the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Russian Federation is a national body responsible for scientific attestation. Among its core functions, VAK establishes requirements for peer-reviewed scientific journals and maintains an official list of journals approved for the publication of dissertation research for the degrees of Candidate of Science and Doctor of Science (PhD). Journals included in the VAK list are subject to editorial oversight and peer review standards. \ExhibitRef{VAK_Polozhenie}

Each venue listed below is included in the VAK register, as reflected on the journals’ official websites and screenshots provided as exhibits. This confirms that the articles were published in recognized scholarly outlets with established editorial governance.

\medskip

\underline{Scientific Paper: ``Methodology for Creating an Effective IT Ecosystem under Resource Constraints''} (A.B.~Taximov, A.A.~Beisenbayev), published in: \emph{Vestnik of Technological University}, Vol.~28, No.~4, 2025.  
\emph{Vestnik of Technological University} is a VAK-listed, peer-reviewed scientific journal publishing research in engineering and information technologies. The article presents a methodology for building operational IT platforms under limited financial and time resources. It addresses technology selection, data organization for real-world workloads, phased system deployment, and management of technical debt to maintain long-term system stability.

\medskip

\underline{Scientific Paper: ``Information Technology Tools to Improve Urban Life: iKomek Experience''} (A.B.~Taximov, D.R.~Satvaldina), published in: \emph{Components of Scientific and Technological Progress}, No.~4(94), 2024.  
\emph{Components of Scientific and Technological Progress} is a VAK-listed, peer-reviewed scientific journal published quarterly. The article analyzes the organizational and technical structure of the iKomek civic contact center, including omnichannel intake, request prioritization, responsibility assignment, and management dashboards. It traces the full lifecycle of a citizen request from initial contact to escalation to the city situation center and identifies architectural elements applicable to other municipalities.

\medskip

\underline{Scientific Paper: ``Geonomics: Address-Registry Maintenance and Population Heat-Map Analytics for Smart-City Operations''} (A.B.~Taximov, A.A.~Beisenbayev), published in: \emph{Engineering Bulletin of the Don}, Issue No.~9, 2024.  
\emph{Engineering Bulletin of the Don} is a VAK-listed, peer-reviewed electronic scientific journal published by the North-Caucasus Research Center of Southern Federal University. The article introduces the Geonomics system, integrating address registries, digital cartography, and government databases into a unified analytical framework. This article explains data standardization, polygon-based spatial analysis, voter-address linkage, and the generation of population heat maps for urban planning and service allocation.

\medskip

\underline{Scientific Paper: ``Possibilities of Using Open Data: the Concept of e-Democracy in Smart-City Management''} (A.B.~Taximov, A.A.~Beisenbayev), published in: \emph{Innovations \& Investments}, No.~11, 2023.  
\emph{Innovations \& Investments} is a VAK-listed, peer-reviewed journal (category K2) focused on economics, management, and innovation. The article examines the role of open data in smart-city governance, tracing the transition from static data publication to controlled API-based access. It discusses regulatory, technical, and organizational practices that enable transparency, civic participation, and safe data reuse by public institutions and external developers.

\medskip

\underline{Scientific Paper: ``Big Data as a Smart-City Management Tool''} (A.B.~Taximov, A.A.~Beisenbayev), published in: \emph{Science and Business: Development Ways}, Issue~1(151), 2024 (Section: Mathematical Modeling and Numerical Methods).  
\emph{Science and Business: Development Ways} is a VAK-listed, peer-reviewed journal used for publishing dissertation research. The article demonstrates how large-scale urban data can be applied as an operational management instrument. It connects data analytics to key performance indicators, planning processes, and resource allocation, describing end-to-end data pipelines and governance mechanisms across transport, social support, and municipal services.

\medskip

\underline{Scientific Paper: ``Information Technologies for Updating and Forming Address Information''} (A.B.~Taximov, A.A.~Beisenbayev), published in: \emph{Herald of Computer and Information Technologies (VKIT)}, 2024, pp.~52--62; DOI: 10.14489/vkit.2024.03.pp.052-062.  
\emph{Herald of Computer and Information Technologies} is a VAK-listed, peer-reviewed monthly journal focused on computer and information technologies. The article describes an election-period data-engineering system that increased voter-list accuracy from 84\% to 100\% through address-code verification, geocoding, normalization, and deduplication. It outlines validation procedures and control mechanisms applicable to similar registry systems.

\underline{Scientific Paper: ``Strategy to Improve the International Transfer of Cybersecurity Technologies''} (A.B.~Taximov), published in: \emph{Automation. Modern Technologies}, 2023.  
\emph{Automation. Modern Technologies} is a VAK-listed, long-running peer-reviewed scientific and technical journal focused on automation and modern information technologies. The article analyzes challenges in cross-border cybersecurity technology transfer under conditions of sanctions and limited access to foreign solutions. It proposes cooperative models involving research centers, industry, and open-source tools, and provides structured recommendations for strengthening national cybersecurity capabilities.

\medskip

\underline{Scientific Paper: ``Faster Decision-Making with Technology to Integrate Artificial Intelligence into Smart-City Control Centers''} (A.B.~Taximov), published in: \emph{Information Systems and Technologies}, 2024.  
\emph{Information Systems and Technologies} is a VAK-listed, peer-reviewed scientific journal of Orel State University focused on information systems and information technologies.
