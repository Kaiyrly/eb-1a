\subsection{Evidence that ~\mrl\ has commanded a high salary relative to others in the field}

\label{subsec:Salary}

The following section presents independent, third-party salary benchmarks for Astana, Republic of Kazakhstan, for the occupation of IT Project Manager. These sources establish baseline market compensation levels in the same geographic labor market and are used for comparison against Mr.~Taximov’s documented earnings.

\medskip

\underline{HR Praktika (Formal Salary Certificate)}

HR Praktika issued a signed salary certificate defining typical job duties and qualification requirements for an IT Project Manager in Astana. The certificate applies an averaging methodology across multiple professional compensation studies (including People Consulting, EY, Habr, and hh.ru) and reflects prevailing base salary levels in the market.

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item Monthly Salary: 500{,}000 KZT
    \item Annualized Total: 6{,}000{,}000 KZT \ExhibitRef{HRPraktikaPMFinal}
\end{itemize}



\medskip

\underline{HeadHunter.kz (Official Letter)}

A signed letter from HeadHunter Kazakhstan reports the 2024 mean monthly salary for IT Project Manager positions in Astana, calculated from vacancies published on its national recruitment platform.

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item Monthly Salary: 442{,}000 KZT
    \item Annualized Total: 5{,}304{,}000 KZT \ExhibitRef{HHPM2024Letter}
\end{itemize}



\medskip

\underline{RecCom Recruiting (Market Overview)}

RecCom Recruiting provided a signed and sealed 2024 market overview for Astana. The overview defines role scope and summarizes compensation data derived from recruitment and placement activity for IT Project Managers.

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item Monthly Salary: 558{,}000 KZT
    \item Annualized Total: 6{,}696{,}000 KZT \ExhibitRef{RecComAstana2024Salaries}
\end{itemize}



\medskip

\underline{Bureau of National Statistics (Official Government Letter)}

The Department of the Bureau of National Statistics for Astana issued official response No.~ЖТ~2025~03546738 (dated 13~October~2025). The data is derived from the nationwide \emph{``Report on the Structure and Distribution of Wages''} (Index~2--T) and is classified under the National Classifier of Occupations (НКЗ) for ICT and project-management roles.

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item Monthly Salary: 758{,}791 KZT
    \item Annualized Total: 9{,}105{,}492 KZT \ExhibitRef{BNSAstanaICTPM2024Letter}
\end{itemize}



\medskip

A formal \emph{Salary Statement} issued by the Municipal State Enterprise iKOMEK109 documents Mr.~Taximov’s compensation for his position titled as a \underline{Head, IT Project Manager} for 2024 \ExhibitRef{IkomekSalaryStatement}:

\begin{center}Total Gross Earnings for 2024: 36{,}280{,}907.9 KZT\end{center}

\textit{The USD equivalent below is provided for reference only and is calculated using the National Bank of Kazakhstan average exchange rate for 2024 (468.99~KZT/USD).}

\[
\frac{36{,}280{,}907.9\ \text{KZT}}{468.99\ \text{KZT/USD}} \approx 77{,}360\ \text{USD}
\]
