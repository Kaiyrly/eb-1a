\section{Summary of \mrls achievements and qualifications}

In this petition, \mrl is presented as an IT Project Manager who has been overseeing the creation and implementation of IT, innovative, Building Information Modelling (BIM), and urban technology systems for more than 10 years.
His work encompasses architectural design, workflow configuration, development management, and product logic formation, enabling the solutions he creates to function reliably in the daily operations of educational, business and government organizations.
In 2008 he graduated from Eurasian National University with honors and he-Got a Bachelor's degree in Construction with specialization in Civil Engineering. In 2015 he earned his Master's degree in Building and Urban Design in Development at University College London (UCL).
His Master's degree dissertation on affordable housing was focused on using IT framework, such as (SDSS) Spatial Decision Support System (see section 2.1.2).

An independent industry expert further confirms that this research-driven foundation translated into practical, nationally relevant urban technology solutions. Kakhar Kashimov states:

\textit{``I believe that Askar's innovative mindset reflects not only his professional experience but also his scientific approach, which he developed through his research work on creating a new mechanism for determining the necessity of constructing social infrastructure facilities such as schools, kindergartens, and residential complexes, using geographic information systems (GIS) and the architecture of Spatial Decision Support Systems (SDSS). In his scientific work, he proposed a unique IT solution to address the problem of inaccurate forecasting and planning of socially significant infrastructure development across the country. I am confident that his IT developments in the field of social infrastructure and industrial system design significantly influence the scaling of information technologies and remain highly relevant and practical today''.}

In 2018 he joined the Nazarbayev University Research and Innovation System (NURIS) as a Director of Technopark.
During that period he improved Technopark as an attractive place for startup companies, small and medium enterprises (SME) by bringing industrial experts, researchers, technology entrepreneurs and investors together. His next joint IT project was the ``Life Signal'' service for Android and iOS platforms, developed with the participation of Google and Apple, and aimed at emergency response and interaction of 112 operational services with citizens' calls, including rational allocation and use of emergency-service resources.

From 2019 to 2021, while working at Astana Innovations, the company responsible for Smart City concept integration Big Data urban platform, the creation of analytical models, and the transition from open data to industrial API services, he was involved in the development of IT solutions that form the basis for the automation of interdepartmental processes and the creation of services for city residents. From March to June 2022, his work at Elorda Qurylys Kompaniyasy, a municipal developer with a public mandate to complete problematic shared construction projects, included the implementation of digital systems for managing budgets, processes, and construction cycles, which required a combination of IT architecture, automation, and management tools. At the same time, starting on January 18, 2022, as an Independent Director of the National Cybersecurity operator ``State Technical Service'' JSC, he participated in strategic oversight and expert work on platforms that protect e-Government and critical information systems.

\mrl{} is currently a Head, \underline{IT project manager} at iKOMEK109 which is similar to the Office of Unified Communications 311 (non-emergency services) in the United States cities. \mrl{} joined iKOMEK109 in May 2022, after getting a solid experience in IT sector and his career centers on \underline{IT Project Management}, \underline{Innovations} and \underline{cybersecurity} in government and enterprises. His résumé \ExhibitRef{Resume} and diplomas \ExhibitRef{Diploma1}\ExhibitRef{Diploma2} are included. Across roles, he has delivered (i) omnichannel civic-service platforms, (ii) election-grade address/registry data pipelines, (iii) national cybersecurity-operations tooling, (iv) computer-vision deployments at city scale and (v) enterprise digitalization programs.

The documents reflect official recognition of his activities, including letters of appreciation from the President of the Republic of Kazakhstan for organizing the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) international summit, conducting presidential and parliamentary elections, numerous awards from government agencies, and recognition from Kaspersky for his contribution to information security (see section 4). Separately, there is confirmation that he was awarded the Bolashak state scholarship to study at UCL, which became the basis for his further professional work in the field of IT Project Management (see section 4.1).

His professional status is reinforced by his membership in the Kazakhstan Information Security Association, where he was accepted by decision of the Council after verification of compliance with the established requirements for professional experience and qualifications (see section 5). The media has repeatedly covered his work on urban systems, digital platforms, and processes for handling requests from residents of the capital. The materials also include translations of these publications and data on the reliability of the sources (see section 6).

A significant part of his professional activity is his work as an expert evaluating projects and scientific research. He is a reviewer at the Universities, a member of the jury for national programs, and a participant in expert commissions at Kaspersky, the city situation center, and the National Security operator. Written conclusions on various materials and proposals are presented, confirming the trust in his professional opinion and the importance of expert assessment in decision-making (see section 7).

The results of his achievements are related to the creation and launch of technological solutions that are successfully used in city services and government structures. These include the iKOMEK109 system, which provides round-the-clock operation and reception of calls from citizens to the unified contact center of the Astana City Administration via telephone number 109 and alternative digital channels, as well as a completely updated version of the iKOMEK 2.0 platform, whose architecture and functionality were developed under his leadership and confirmed in documents and public materials (see sections 3.1, 3.1.1, 3.1.2). A significant milestone was the introduction of the Geonomics information system, which enabled the city of Astana to achieve maximum accuracy in compiling voter lists within its territory by bringing together address data on citizens' places of residence into a single structure, as reflected in publications and confirmed by independent sources (see section 10). The materials also include projects on computer vision, telematics, and network monitoring, under which solutions that have proven their practical value were created and implemented under his leadership (see section 8).

Also presented are his scientific articles on urban analytics, data management, digital services, and security issues, published in peer-reviewed journals from the Higher Attestation Commission list.
